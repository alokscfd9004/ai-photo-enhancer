About

AI Photo Enhancer

A self-hosted photo super-resolution studio powered by the open-source Real-ESRGAN engine (NCNN Vulkan build). Upload a low-resolution image and get a clean, sharp HD or 4K reconstruction in seconds.

How it works

  1. Your photo is validated (type, size, pixel budget) and stored locally.
  2. A background worker invokes realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan with the model you picked.
  3. The upscaled PNG is written to media/enhanced/ and shown with a before/after comparator.

Models

  • RealESRGAN x4plus — best all-round quality for real photos.
  • RealESRGAN x4plus anime — tuned for illustrations and line art.
  • RealESR AnimeVideo v3 — fast, supports 2×/3×/4×.

The noise cleanup slider applies a gentle median pre-filter to reduce sensor noise and JPEG artifacts before super-resolution.
